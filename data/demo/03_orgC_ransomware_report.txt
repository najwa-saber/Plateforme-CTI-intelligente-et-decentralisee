Ransomware intrusion at a logistics provider (synthetic exercise)

The Driftlock ransomware encrypted the file servers and the backup appliance of a logistics provider over a
weekend. The operators deleted volume shadow copies with vssadmin, stopped the database services and dropped a
ransom note named HOW_TO_RESTORE.txt. The locker binary has SHA-256
3a7bd3e2360a3d29eea436fcfb7e44c735d117c42d1c1835420b6b9942dd4f1b and communicated with 198.51.100.240 before
encryption. Initial access is suspected to come from stolen VPN credentials. The ransom note points to
driftlock-recovery.test and requests payment in bitcoin within 72 hours.
