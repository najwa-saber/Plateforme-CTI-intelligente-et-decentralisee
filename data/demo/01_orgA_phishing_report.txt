Phishing campaign impersonating the HR self-service portal

Summary: Since 9 November 2026, employees of several engineering companies have received emails announcing
an "annual salary review" that required them to sign a document on the HR portal. The emails were sent from
hr-notifications@secure-hr-portal.example and contained a link to hxxps://secure-hr-portal[.]example/sso/login,
a cloned single sign-on page. Credentials entered on the fake login page were posted to a collector hosted on
203.0.113.66. A second wave used an HTML attachment (Salary_Review_2026.html, MD5 9e107d9d372bb6826bd81d3542a419d6)
that rendered the same fake portal locally to evade URL filtering.

The domain secure-hr-portal.example was registered three days before the first emails. Stolen accounts were
later used to send internal phishing messages to colleagues. No malware was dropped: the objective is
credential harvesting. Recommended actions: block the domain and the IP address, reset the passwords of users
who clicked the link, and search mail logs for the sender domain.
