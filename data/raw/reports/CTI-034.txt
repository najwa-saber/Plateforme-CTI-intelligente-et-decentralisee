Ransomware spread through remote desktop

Attackers brute-forced an internet-facing remote desktop server, created a local administrator and spread to the file servers. A locker dubbed Mirelock encrypted 2 TB of documents. The binary MD5 is 0df004b3cd92c15d82357f9a389bcd3b. The note directed victims to http://mirelock-decrypt.test/id. Remote desktop exposure remains a frequent root cause of ransomware incidents in small organisations.
