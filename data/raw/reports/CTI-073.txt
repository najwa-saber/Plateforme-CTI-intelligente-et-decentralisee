Exploitation of a collaboration server template injection

A server-side template injection in an enterprise wiki (CVE-2099-1004) allows unauthenticated attackers to execute code. Shortly after the advisory, exploitation started from 203.0.113.250. The attackers created administrator accounts and installed a cryptocurrency miner in a few cases, but most activity consisted of reconnaissance. The exploit request targets the endpoint https://wiki.corp-intra.test/template/aui/text-inline.vm.
