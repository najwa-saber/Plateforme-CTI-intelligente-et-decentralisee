QR code phishing in fake parking fines

Printed notices placed on car windshields asked drivers to scan a QR code to pay a parking fine. The code opened https://city-parking-pay.example/fine?ref=55A1 on the mobile phone, outside corporate protections. The page requested card data and a mobile banking password. Email variants of the same lure embedded the QR code in a PDF attachment so that secure email gateways could not inspect the link. The domain was hosted on 192.0.2.201 and used a free TLS certificate.
