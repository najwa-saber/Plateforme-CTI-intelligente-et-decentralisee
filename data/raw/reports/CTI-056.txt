Cryptominer spreading through exposed container APIs

Attackers scan for container management APIs exposed without authentication and start containers running a cryptocurrency miner. The image downloads the miner from 198.51.100.101 and connects to pool-proxy-relay.example. The miner binary SHA-256 2837117cca6267e1ad5344abe287a68e9b0009fb74b27e5b0d5cad97af6b9eef is shared with the SSH botnet described earlier.
