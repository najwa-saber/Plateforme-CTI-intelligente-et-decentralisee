Exploitation of Acme VPN gateway authentication bypass

A critical authentication bypass in the Acme VPN gateway (CVE-2099-0142) is actively exploited. A crafted request to the management interface returns a valid session without credentials. Attackers then upload a web shell to https://vpn.victim-gw.example/dana-na/auth/shell.cgi. Mass exploitation attempts came from 198.51.100.140. Administrators must apply the vendor patch and check the integrity of the appliance.
