StealRay information stealer analysis

StealRay is a commodity information stealer sold as a subscription. Once executed it collects saved browser passwords, cookies, cryptocurrency wallet files and screenshots, compresses them and uploads the archive over HTTPS to cdn-telemetry-sync.example. The analysed sample has SHA-256 97d5bba19c2dc2af1f218286daaacb3e3a5e6d8a98b1fb97724c0fd54c8f0e4f. The C2 server 203.0.113.88 also serves configuration updates. The malware deletes itself after exfiltration, leaving few traces on disk.
