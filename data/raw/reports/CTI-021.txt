NightVault ransomware intrusion at a regional hospital

The NightVault operators encrypted file servers of a regional hospital after a two-week dwell time. Before encryption, they deleted volume shadow copies with vssadmin and stopped backup services. Around 300 GB of data were exfiltrated to cloud storage, then a ransom note threatened publication on a leak site. The encryptor binary had SHA-256 ab12b8a1a7f51f0b57174e86bf6bc35ea403a35542c95c5dd5941ec371ebb1b1 and appended the extension .nvault to files. Command-and-control traffic was observed to 198.51.100.77.
