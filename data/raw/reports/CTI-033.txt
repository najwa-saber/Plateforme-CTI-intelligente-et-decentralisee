NightVault decryptor reliability issues

Victims who paid the NightVault ransom reported that the decryptor failed on large databases, leaving files corrupted. Incident responders recommend testing decryptors on copies only. The encryptor sample ab12b8a1a7f51f0b57174e86bf6bc35ea403a35542c95c5dd5941ec371ebb1b1 is still used by most affiliates, and the C2 198.51.100.77 remains active.
