Phishing through collaboration chat messages

External tenants sent chat messages to employees claiming to be the internal IT team. The messages explained that the mailbox was full and requested the user to re-authenticate via https://teams-it-support.example/mailbox. Because external chat was allowed by default, the messages were delivered without warning banners. The landing page harvested credentials and forwarded them to 203.0.113.201. Restricting external chat federation reduced the exposure.
