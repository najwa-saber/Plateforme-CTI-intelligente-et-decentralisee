StealRay panel exposed

Researchers found an exposed StealRay administration panel on 203.0.113.88. It contained logs from 4,000 victims including browser passwords and session cookies. The panel software matches the stealer sample 97d5bba19c2dc2af1f218286daaacb3e3a5e6d8a98b1fb97724c0fd54c8f0e4f. Affected companies were notified through national CERTs.
