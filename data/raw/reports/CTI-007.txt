Session token theft linked to M365 phishing kit

Incident responders investigating unauthorised mailbox access traced the initial event to a document-sharing email. The victim authenticated on a proxied page served by login-m365-verify.example, which captured the session cookie. A second hostname, sso-m365-verify.example, was registered by the same actor. Both resolved to 203.0.113.45. The intruder downloaded contact lists and sent internal phishing to colleagues. Revoking refresh tokens and resetting credentials stopped the activity.
