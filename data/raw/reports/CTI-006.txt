Voice phishing targeting help desk staff

Callers impersonating employees contacted the IT help desk and requested a password reset and the enrolment of a new MFA device. They used personal details gathered from social networks to answer verification questions. After the reset, the attacker logged in from 203.0.113.150 and searched the mailbox for payment information. The same group later sent follow-up emails from it-helpdesk-support.test asking other staff to approve push notifications. Help desks should enforce call-back verification.
